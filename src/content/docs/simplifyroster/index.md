SimplifyRoster is a shift scheduling platform for support teams that run around
the clock across more than one country. It holds the schedule, the leave, the
public holidays and the overtime in one place, so "who is covering 3 AM on
Thursday?" has an answer you can see rather than reconstruct.

## Start here

- **Quick start** — from sign-up to a working schedule in about five minutes.
- **Workspace setup** — creating a workspace, inviting your team, and what each
  of the four roles can see.
- **Agents** — adding agents, setting regular working hours, and linking an
  agent to the person who logs in as them.

## The schedule

- **Day view** — the 24-hour timeline, the live clock, and how uncovered hours
  are flagged.
- **Monthly calendar** — coverage at a month's range, and the badges that mark
  an undercovered day.
- **CSV import** — bringing an existing rota in rather than typing it twice.

## Time off

- **Holiday groups** — one group per country, each following its own national
  calendar, and how a group's holiday is excluded from its agents' shifts.
- **Leave** — requesting, approving, and how an approved day appears on the
  schedule.

## Overtime

- **Assignment** — filling a gap from the day view, including which agents are
  offered and why agents on a holiday are marked amber.
- **Approvals** — the request and review flow, and where approved hours are
  counted.

## Reports and visibility

- **Work reports** — templates, submission, the manager view, and the
  missed-report tracker.
- **Leaderboard** — what the composite score is built from.
- **Who's online** — how presence is derived, and who can see it.

## The assistant

- **Using it** — the kinds of question it can answer about your live schedule.
- **Setup** — supplying a key, and turning it on per workspace.
