SimplifyGuide is a free WordPress plugin that turns a task you click through
once into a step-by-step guide with screenshots — and then walks other people
through the same task on their own screen with **Show me**.

It runs entirely on your site. Guides are WordPress posts, screenshots and
narration live in your Media Library, and nothing leaves your site unless you
turn on AI writing or transcription with a provider and key you choose.

## Quick start

1. **Install and activate.** The setup wizard opens: choose what Smart Blur
   hides, your brand colour, whether to use AI, and who sees new guides. You
   can run it again from **SimplifyGuide → Setup Wizard**.
2. **Record.** Go to **SimplifyGuide → Record a Guide**, or press **Record
   guide** in the toolbar on any screen. Press **Start recording**, share the
   tab when your browser asks, and do the task. Each click and field becomes a
   step.
3. **Finish.** Press **Finish**. The guide opens in the editor, already named
   from what you did. Tidy the wording, add arrows or blur, then **Publish**.
4. **Share.** Readers find it in the Help Center and press **Show me** to be
   walked through it live.

The [first-guide tutorial](/product/simplifyguide/docs/first-guide/) does all
four with a real task.
