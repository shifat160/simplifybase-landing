SimplifyGuide is a free WordPress plugin that turns a task you click through
once into a step-by-step guide with screenshots — and then walks other people
through the same task on their own screen.

It runs entirely on your site. Guides are WordPress posts, screenshots live in
your Media Library, and AI writing is optional and uses the provider and key
you choose.

## Quick start

1. **Install and activate** the plugin. The setup wizard opens: choose what
   Smart Blur hides, your brand colour, whether to use AI, and who can see new
   guides. It takes about two minutes and can be run again from
   **SimplifyGuide → Setup Wizard**.
2. **Record.** Go to **SimplifyGuide → Record a Guide**, or press **Record
   guide** in the toolbar on any screen. Share the tab when your browser asks —
   that is how screenshots are taken — then do the task. Each click and field
   becomes a step.
3. **Finish.** Press **Finish**. The guide opens in the editor, already named
   from what you did. Tidy the wording, add arrows or blur, then publish.
4. **Share.** Readers find it in the Help Center and press **Show me** to be
   walked through it live. You can also embed it, share a public link, or
   download it as video, GIF, PDF, Word, HTML, Markdown or JSON.
