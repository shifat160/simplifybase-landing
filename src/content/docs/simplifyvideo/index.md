SimplifyVideo is in design. This page exists so the documentation URL is stable
from day one — bookmark it and it will fill in rather than move.

## What it will cover

- **The brief** — what to write, which screenshots are worth supplying, and how
  much detail actually changes the storyboard you get back.
- **Editing scenes** — reordering, rewriting, swapping shots, and cutting a beat
  without the ones after it drifting out of time.
- **The brand kit** — logo, colours and typeface, how they are applied to every
  scene, and where a per-video override is allowed.
- **Templates** — what each one is for and the shape of story it suits.
- **Formats and export** — aspect ratios, resolution, what a render costs and
  what happens when one fails.
- **Catalogue sources** — reading product shots, names and prices from
  WooCommerce, and re-exporting when they change.

## Shaping it

The open question is which templates earn their place. If there is a video you
keep meaning to make and keep not making, the reason you have not made it is
the most useful thing you could tell us.
